# Channel Brief — Kids Educational YouTube

## Identity

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Niche** | Kids Education — Alphabet, Numbers, STEM Basics |
| **Target Age** | 2–6 years (toddlers to pre-K/Kindergarten) |
| **Tone** | Warm, gentle, encouraging, simple, repetitive for retention |
| **Pace** | Slightly slow and clear; short sentences; repetition is a feature |
| **Language Level** | 2–4 word sentences where possible; concrete nouns; no idioms |
| **YouTube Designation** | Made for Kids (COPPA-compliant; disables comments & personalized ads) |
| **Upload Cadence** | TBD — suggest 2–3 videos/week once pipeline is running |

---

## Content Pillars

### 1. Alphabet (Episodes 1–26 minimum)
- One letter per episode
- Format: Letter name → Letter sound → 3–5 words starting with that letter → simple song → recap
- Example: "Letter A — Apple, Ant, Alligator 🐊"

### 2. Numbers (Episodes 1–20 minimum)
- One number or range per episode
- Format: Count objects → write number → count again → simple song → recap
- Example: "Counting 1 to 5 — Let's Count Together!"

### 3. STEM Basics (Open-ended series)
- Simple science concepts a 3-year-old can grasp
- Format: Question → simple explanation → visual demo → recap question
- Example: "What is a Shadow?", "Why is the Sky Blue?", "What do Plants Need?"

### 4. Shapes & Colors (Bonus series)
- Basic shapes, colors, and combinations
- Example: "Circle, Square, Triangle — Shapes Song"

## Visual Style

- **Colors:** High-contrast, primary and bright secondary colors (red, blue, yellow, green, orange, purple)
- **Shapes:** Simple, rounded, child-friendly
- **On-screen text:** Large, bold, easy-to-read font; text matches spoken words (supports early literacy)
- **Animation style:** TBD — reuse Veo/Flow pipeline from Qasas ul Ambiya if style fits, or commission new style
- **Background:** Clean, uncluttered; one or two elements per scene
- **Consistency:** Same character design, same font, same color palette across ALL episodes

---

## Audio Style

- **Voice:** Warm, clear, slightly slow, enthusiastic but calm
- **Music:** Original or royalty-free only; simple, repetitive melodies
- **Sound effects:** Minimal, friendly, not startling
- **Volume balance:** Voice clearly above music at all times

## SEO & Discovery Notes

- Parents search for this content, not the kids — write titles/descriptions for parents
- Key search phrases: "letter [X] for kids", "counting for toddlers", "ABC song", "numbers 1-10", "simple science for kids"
- Playlists matter: Create "Alphabet Series", "Counting Series", "STEM for Kids" playlists
- Thumbnails must communicate the concept instantly — large letter/number, bright color, friendly face

---

## Monetization & Revenue Notes

> **IMPORTANT:** Made-for-Kids content has lower CPMs than general-audience content due to COPPA restrictions on personalized advertising. Do not plan revenue around standard CPM rates.

Revenue levers (outside AdSense):
- Channel memberships (if eligibility unlocks and COPPA allows in future)
- Merchandise (plush toys, flash cards) — long-term
- Educational licensing — long-term
- Sponsorships from family-friendly, COPPA-compliant brands — check YouTube policies carefully

---

## What This Channel Does NOT Do

- No auto-upload without human approval
- No promises of specific view counts, subscriber counts, or revenue
- No copyrighted music, characters, or third-party IP
- No content targeting children for data collection
- No comments section (disabled under Made-for-Kids)
